Below the Record

How to move around

Mouse
Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click a star to read it.
→ ←
Next and previous tour stop. Page Down and Page Up do the same, so a presenter clicker works.
Home End
First and last tour stop.
Esc
Close the panel.
Tab
Move through the buttons. All stars lists every star as a button.
L
Show or hide the names of the references.
+ −
Larger or smaller panel text.
U
Hide or show the buttons, for a clean stage.
F
Full screen.
In the headset
Point and pull the trigger on a star. A or right grip: next stop. B or left grip: back. Trigger on empty sky closes the panel.

Guided tour: press → to begin
